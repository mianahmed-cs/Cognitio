Cognitio leverages the native HTML5 Web Storage API (`localStorage`). All courses and study tasks are serialized to JSON strings and persisted locally in your browser. This ensures 100% student privacy, instant speed, and survival across page refreshes during evaluation.
To prevent cognitive clutter, Cognitio avoids pre-seeded dummy subjects. Students begin with a clean slate and use the "Add Course" modal to configure their exact semester syllabus with personalized color tags, credit hours, and schedule times.
