Proven Study Wisdom

Strategies from top achievers.

Actionable productivity insights to guide your daily revision cycles.

Active Recall

"Testing yourself repeatedly beats re-reading notes 10 to 1. Before reviewing a lecture, write down everything you remember on a blank page."

CS

Dr. Barbara Oakley

Author of 'A Mind for Numbers' & Learning How to Learn

Time-Blocking

"Decide what you are going to work on before you open your laptop. A 25-minute sprint with zero notification tabs accomplishes more than three hours of distracted multi-tasking."

CN

Cal Newport

Computer Science Professor, Georgetown University

The Feynman Technique

"If you cannot explain a data structure or algorithm to an eight-year-old in plain language, you have not truly mastered it. Identify the gaps in your explanation."

RF

Richard Feynman

Theoretical Physicist & Nobel Laureate

"Memory decays exponentially unless reinforced at calculated intervals. Review key concepts on day 1, day 3, day 7, and day 21 to lock them into long-term retention."

HE

Hermann Ebbinghaus

Pioneer in Cognitive Psychology & Memory
